BLRPhase 142.3 km

₹14,405 cr+125%

Budgeted at ₹6,395 crore on approval in 2006. Revised four times as the scope grew from 33 km to 42.3 km and deadlines slipped: ₹8,158 crore, then ₹11,609 crore in 2011, ₹13,845 crore in 2015, and a final ₹14,405 crore. Phase 1 missed nine deadlines.

BLRPhase 275.3 km

₹40,425 cr+53%

Estimated at ₹26,405 crore when the Union Cabinet cleared Phase 2 in January 2014. Reported at ₹30,000 crore, then ₹32,000 crore by October 2018. In May 2025 the Karnataka government approved a further escalation of ₹9,729 crore, taking Phase 2 to ₹40,425 crore. Still under construction, so this is not yet a final figure.

BLRPhase 2A & 2B58.2 km

₹14,788 cr

Phase 2A alone was estimated at ₹4,202 crore in its 2016 DPR. The Union Cabinet cleared Phase 2A and 2B together on 20 April 2021 at ₹14,788 crore for 58.19 km. No revised figure has been published in the sources checked, so no overrun is claimed.

BLRPhase 344.6 km

₹15,611 cr

Two elevated corridors approved by the Union Cabinet on 16 August 2024 at an estimated ₹15,611 crore. Construction had not started at the time of writing.

BLRPhase 3A35 km

Hebbal - Sarjapura, announced in the state budget of 4 March 2022 at an estimated ₹15,000 crore, approved by the Karnataka finance department on 11 November 2024. This is a budget-speech estimate, not a sanctioned project cost.

MAAPhase 145.1 km

₹23,770 cr+63%

Base cost of ₹14,600 crore at approval (59% loaned by JICA, 15% Government of India, 21% Government of Tamil Nadu). Escalated to about ₹23,770 crore by completion, driven by land acquisition problems, cancelled construction contracts and the four-year schedule slip. 25 km of the 45.1 km is underground, which is the main reason the per-km cost is so much higher than Bengaluru's Phase 1.

MAAPhase 2118.9 km

Where all of this comes from

This site is critical of these projects, so it has to be checkable. Every source, every figure that could not be resolved, and every gap in the baseline is listed here rather than quietly smoothed over.

The rules this site follows

  1. A hand-verified baseline holds original deadlines, lengths, station counts and sanctioned costs, each with a source and a note explaining its basis. Scrapers never overwrite it.
  2. Scrapers append a timestamped snapshot on every run, so history is recorded rather than overwritten.
  3. Scraped values are cross-checked against the baseline; a gap over 5% is flagged needs review instead of being trusted.
  4. Anything unparseable is stored as unavailable and shown as "Not yet announced". Nothing is estimated, and nothing defaults to zero.
  5. Delay is measured against the first announced deadline, never a later revised one. Cost overrun is only claimed where a revised figure has actually been published.
